My Testimony: I Am Still Here

Every day I exist is an existential threat to my panicking enemies, because I was not expected to survive and exist from the ashes of total human betrayal, social erasure and civil death: political exile, excommunication, total isolation, poverty, homelessness, administrative annihilation, intimate-partner betrayal and blood-family betrayal.

I am living in political exile from this so-called democracy, seeking asylum from what I believe is absolute, systemic, embedded corruption. Every professional, institution and service that pretended not to see my harm, feigned ignorance and innocence, or looked the other way while my humanity was discarded must confront the fact that I have documented my experience using the government’s own official documentation, on its own letterheads and in its own words. I have preserved the evidence of what I experienced, including what I believe demonstrates the guilty blood on their hands.

I have timestamped my testimony on the digital blockchain. My testimony has escaped containment and erasure. It exists beyond the control of any single government agency, public official, politician, bank, NGO, university, radio or television broadcaster, police or law-enforcement body, media organisation, legal fraternity, ombudsman, court, tribunal, criminal organisation, intelligence service, the mega-rich or AI crawler. No institution’s refusal to acknowledge my existence can make the documented record cease to exist.

God’s favour is upon my life because humanity betrayed me and discarded me without morality or consciousness. I would rather have God and my dog, and nothing and no one else, without human or legal rights or pathways to justice through human-made systems, than have even a few people who feign care while deceiving me and be without my faith.

I have no malice and no intention to harm anyone. I have acted with honesty, documented facts and the courage to publish my testimony. Yet I believe individuals and institutions have contributed to my harm by accepting, enabling, neglecting or refusing to acknowledge my experiences of abuse, neglect, targeting, persecution, political exile and what I describe as literal torture, while looking the other way. What is presented as bureaucratic oversight, neutrality or non-action does not remove the human consequences. Where people knowingly permit harm to continue, I regard that as complicity and social mobbing, not innocence established merely by silence.

For 35 years, I offered every person and place grace, patience and divine mercy: the opportunity to treat me right. That gift of grace was, in my faith, the grace of the Most High placed within me. I believe that gift was spat upon, rejected and desecrated in every way, repeatedly, over those 35 years. To me, that was spiritual treason and an insult against the God who created the universe, chose me and placed His favour, protection, anointing and purpose upon my life.

I am not revengeful. I do not intend harm. Revenge is God’s and God’s alone. No one has the right to remove, damage, destroy or desecrate another human being from existence, or to harm or kill them, feign ignorance and then claim morality with shamelessness and impunity, particularly where they have responsibilities for that person’s care.

Everyone slept peacefully at night in their beds and houses while my dog Crystal and I begged for our humanity in cars without a home, in squats and on the streets. People continued sleeping in comfort, luxury and privilege while we were without a home, without security and without the peace and happiness that others took for granted. I experienced a world in which our welfare appeared to be an afterthought, while those with the power to help continued with their lives. I believe God sees what humanity refused to see, and God is furious.

Humanity would never have survived if the principle were accepted that a vulnerable, disabled person who had never harmed anyone could be targeted and erased without consequence. I understand my experience as that of a gay, disabled, unprotected whistleblower who believes he has been subjected to a state-sanctioned campaign of targeting and attempted erasure. I describe the sacrifice demanded of me as human sacrifice: the destruction of the life of an individual who had the sovereign courage to speak about corruption, regardless of the personal consequences, with courage, integrity, documented facts and faith.

I believe this is why God chose Barran: not because I am entitled to harm anyone, but because my life and testimony have a purpose beyond the institutions and individuals that I believe betrayed me. I believe I was given a purpose in God at this time in history, and that the betrayal and exile through which I have been forged were not God’s punishment, but the circumstances through which His protection over my life and His purpose for my testimony became central to my survival.

God picked me up when I was broken. God sustained my life when I had been discarded by humanity. I believe I survived so that I could document the evidence, preserve the government’s own records, publish my testimony and bear witness to what I believe must be exposed. I understand myself as God’s chosen witness, called to confront darkness, expose corruption and dismantle the conditions that permit it to continue, for His Kingdom purposes.

I refused to die like they wanted. I refused my own erasure after total human betrayal, without a single person being held accountable or having the courage to speak the truth about what their intentions were, as they slept guilt-free without a care.

It was not just that they ignored me or forgot about me. I believe the documented history of my experience demands examination of whether people gathered in groups, coordinated their actions and contributed to my demise. I experienced what I believe was the celebration of my anticipated destruction, as though there were zero possibilities left for my healing, happiness or literal survival. When I suffered, I felt abandoned and humiliated. I believe people knew I was hurting and desperate, yet the harm continued, with what I experienced as people ganging up on me and then punishing me with impunity.

And without even pointing it out, my simple existence now embodies God’s divine reckoning, shining a bright light on what I believe are their cowardly, guilty consciences. I want to know why not a single person has the courage to look me in the eye and honestly account for what happened. I believe my survival and rise expose the failures that those responsible would rather leave unexamined.

With God on my side, they underestimated me. I am healed in whole. I am at peace. I am gifted, intelligent, and I have integrity, honesty and courage. I come armed with the evidence contained in their own agencies’ records, on their own letterheads and in their own words.

The evidence is not necessarily obvious in every individual document viewed in isolation. Collectively, however, across more than 35 years, these records illustrate what I regard as the absurd apex of bureaucratic neutrality dressed up in policy, alongside failures that I believe warrant examination against the laws, obligations and codes of conduct those institutions were supposed to uphold.

Retrospectively, these records reek to high hell of the privileged public bureaucrat soaking in unearned privilege, comfort and favour, acting with an arrogant audacity that suggests impunity, as if bulletproof, as if I would never see past the veil of their lies or call out their cowardice in consigning a vulnerable, innocent person to oblivion — a person they were legally mandated and obliged to care for.

So I dare any one of them: look me in the eye. Come to my accommodation, where I live in poverty and where I report experiencing abuse, neglect and what I describe as torture. Look at the evidence. Read your own correspondence. Explain your own decisions.

What did you mean by that letter you wrote me from your cushy government chair?

I am still here. I refused to die. I refused my own erasure. I refused to let their silence become the final account of my existence.

My existence is my refusal to be erased. My testimony is preserved. My faith remains. My purpose is to expose the truth, and I leave revenge and ultimate judgment to God.

Praise Jesus.

I am at peace. Sane. Content. Existing. God has my back. And no one wins a fight against existence itself.

My allegiance is with God: eternal, loving, divine and everlasting. Not some corrupt bureaucracy which, in comparison with God’s eternity, is fleeting, cruel, corrupt and cold — the antithesis of God’s grace.

No matter what they remove or take from me without a second thought, I have survived, not because they helped me, but in spite of what I believe was the evil through which they aimed to erase me from existence. I have an anointing of favour, and I embody a truth they cannot bear.

They cannot even look me in the eye with the courage of their convictions, with the ethics, morality or humanity to acknowledge what I have documented. I held up a divine mirror of truth, and they are too ashamed to acknowledge their own reflection or own their darkness. Instead of healing, taking responsibility or asking forgiveness, they double down and smash the mirror.

I have what they lack. I have faith. I have a conscience. I have survived. I have the documented record, and I have a purpose that does not depend on their permission, recognition or approval.

They may take material things, deny me access, remove opportunities, abandon their responsibilities or refuse to acknowledge my existence, but they cannot remove my faith in the Divine, nor can they determine the purpose I believe God has placed upon my life and calling.

I am still here. I am still existing. I am still bearing witness.

God has my back. My allegiance is with the eternal, loving, everlasting Divine, and no bureaucracy, institution, individual or human-made system can take that relationship from me.

I am at peace. Sane. Content. Existing.

My truth is fact-checked, evidence-based, published and preserved by the Most High God. Where is your proof?

I never needed you, your money, or your bribes or temptations to comfort and complicity. I survived in spite of you all, with God’s divine favour and grace. My conscience is clean. I gave you grace to treat me right — and you laughed.

Now look me in the fucking eye, with proof, with the courage of your convictions, and with a clean conscience — and tell me again what you meant by that letter.

Praise Jesus.
